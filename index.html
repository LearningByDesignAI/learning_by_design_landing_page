The AI ROI Matrix

Where AI returns start to compound.

Most teams have the tools. Returns compound only when fluent people, all the way up to leadership, work on shared systems. That's why AI transformation is a human agency problem disguised as a technology problem.

Up means more fluent people, all the way to leadership. Right means more shared systems.

People: AI fluency and leadership ↑

Skilled but stuck

A few highly capable people or teams. Little shared cross-functional infrastructure.

  • Power users with their own prompts, projects and skills
  • ROI stays inside individual or small-team workflows

Compounding systems

Fluent at every level, running on one shared brain.

  • Power users in key leadership roles, with a mandate
  • A brain (context layer) that every agent, skill, policy and access rule runs on

Scattered pilots

Seats bought, usage uneven.

  • Low fluency and few systems, especially outside engineering and R&D
  • Utilization gets tracked. Value doesn't.

Agents without a brain

Agents built. Nothing compounds.

  • Outside teams shipped agents that sit apart from each other
  • No shared context, and internal systems never mapped well enough to know what to build next
