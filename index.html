The uncomfortable truth

AI transformation is a human agency problem disguised as a technology problem.

Most teams have the tools. Engagement is tracked. What it produced is not. AI pays off when it amplifies your people's judgment, not when it extracts it to cut headcount. Amplifying > Extracting.

Our goal: close the agency gap in 1,000 companies by 2030, without a single AI-driven layoff.
